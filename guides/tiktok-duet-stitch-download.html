How to Download TikTok Duets and Stitches

Save duets and stitches, and understand what makes them different from normal videos.

Duets and stitches are TikTok's collaborative formats: a duet plays two videos side by side, while a stitch starts with someone else's clip and continues it. Downloading them works much like downloading a normal video, but there are a few quirks, because the clip contains more than one creator's work. This guide explains the method and the etiquette.

Download a duet

Open the duet, tap Share, then Copy Link, and paste the link into a downloader. The downloaded file should contain the full side-by-side layout, exactly as you saw it. If the tool returns only one side, try a different downloader, because some tools handle duets better than others.

Download a stitch

A stitch is a single video that begins with a borrowed clip, so a standard downloader usually handles it well. Copy the link, paste it into the tool, and download the MP4. Check the start of the downloaded clip to confirm the stitched portion is included.

Check the creator's settings

Duets and stitches can be disabled by the original creator, and if they are, the video may not be downloadable. Respect that setting. If you collaborated on the duet, you can also download your own version from your profile, where your own recording is stored.

Credit both creators

A duet or stitch involves at least two people. If you share the downloaded clip anywhere, credit both the original creator and the person who duetted or stitched it. Linking to both originals is the fairest way to share collaborative work.

Putting it into practice

Start with a single, short example and work through the steps above from beginning to end. Do not try to do everything at once, and do not use an important or irreplaceable video for your first attempt. Once the first try works, repeat the process with a second example, and you will find the steps become familiar quickly. Keep your files organised as you go, give them clear names, and check the finished result on the device where you plan to use it, whether that is your phone, your computer, or a tablet.

Common mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is rushing: pasting the wrong link, picking the lowest quality, or downloading from the first site that appears in a search without checking whether it is trustworthy. The second is ignoring storage and file names, which leaves you with a folder full of files called download-1 and download-2 that you cannot tell apart. The third is forgetting the creator: always credit the person who made the original, keep personal copies personal, and ask before you reuse someone else's work in public.
