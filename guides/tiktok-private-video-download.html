Can You Download a Private TikTok Video?

What is possible — and what is not — when a TikTok video is private or restricted.

Private TikTok videos raise a common question: can you still download them? The short answer is usually no, and that is by design. TikTok's privacy settings exist so creators can control who sees their content. This guide explains what private means, what downloaders can and cannot do, and the legitimate ways to get a copy when you have permission.

What private means on TikTok

A private video is visible only to the creator, while a friends-only video is visible to approved followers. A public video from a private account is visible only to that account's followers. In all these cases, TikTok does not expose the video file to the public, so outside tools cannot fetch it.

Why downloaders fail on private videos

Downloaders work by reading the public page for a video. If that page requires a login or a follow, the tool sees an error instead of a video. No reputable downloader can bypass this, and any tool that claims it can is likely a scam or a phishing attempt. Do not enter your TikTok password into such a tool.

Legitimate ways to get a copy

If you are the creator, you can download your own video from your profile or drafts. If you are a friend or collaborator, ask the creator to send you the original file, or to change the video's privacy temporarily. For your own private videos, the built-in save option in the app is the simplest route.

Respect privacy settings

Privacy settings are a creator's choice, not a puzzle to solve. Trying to bypass them can breach TikTok's terms and damage trust. If a video is private, treat it as unavailable unless the creator shares it with you directly.

Putting it into practice

Start with a single, short example and work through the steps above from beginning to end. Do not try to do everything at once, and do not use an important or irreplaceable video for your first attempt. Once the first try works, repeat the process with a second example, and you will find the steps become familiar quickly. Keep your files organised as you go, give them clear names, and check the finished result on the device where you plan to use it, whether that is your phone, your computer, or a tablet.

Common mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is rushing: pasting the wrong link, picking the lowest quality, or downloading from the first site that appears in a search without checking whether it is trustworthy. The second is ignoring storage and file names, which leaves you with a folder full of files called download-1 and download-2 that you cannot tell apart. The third is forgetting the creator: always credit the person who made the original, keep personal copies personal, and ask before you reuse someone else's work in public.
