How to Download TikTok Audio as MP3

Extract the sound from any TikTok video and save it as an MP3 file.

TikTok is full of sounds worth keeping: a catchy song, a funny voiceover, a podcast clip, or a sound you want to use in your own edit. Downloading just the audio saves space and lets you listen without the video. This guide shows how to extract the audio as an MP3, on phone and computer, and how to use it responsibly.

Get the video link

Find the video with the sound you want, tap Share, then Copy Link. The audio extractor needs the same link a video downloader uses, because it first reads the video and then separates the sound track. A short, clear link works best; double-check you copied the right video.

Extract the MP3

Paste the link into an audio downloader or a video downloader that offers an audio option, and choose MP3. The tool should show the sound title and duration before you download. If it offers several bitrates, pick the highest one for the best sound quality.

Save and play the audio

On a phone, the MP3 usually lands in Downloads or Files; on a computer, it goes to your Downloads folder. You can play it in any music app, add it to a playlist, or import it into a video editor. Rename the file with the song or sound name so it is easy to find later.

Use audio fairly

A sound from TikTok may be copyrighted music or someone else's original audio. Keep it for personal listening, learning, or editing your own non-commercial clips, and credit the creator when you share anything made with it. Do not sell the audio or claim it as your own work.

Putting it into practice

Start with a single, short example and work through the steps above from beginning to end. Do not try to do everything at once, and do not use an important or irreplaceable video for your first attempt. Once the first try works, repeat the process with a second example, and you will find the steps become familiar quickly. Keep your files organised as you go, give them clear names, and check the finished result on the device where you plan to use it, whether that is your phone, your computer, or a tablet.

Common mistakes to avoid

The most common mistake is rushing: pasting the wrong link, picking the lowest quality, or downloading from the first site that appears in a search without checking whether it is trustworthy. The second is ignoring storage and file names, which leaves you with a folder full of files called download-1 and download-2 that you cannot tell apart. The third is forgetting the creator: always credit the person who made the original, keep personal copies personal, and ask before you reuse someone else's work in public.
